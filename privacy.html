Privacy

Images uploaded through this tool are stored by the configured image-storage provider and may be publicly accessible through their generated URL. Anyone who has the URL can view the image — the same as any public CDN link. Do not upload images you want to keep private.

What we don't say

We will never claim your images are "never stored." Permanent public hosting is the whole point of this tool — the image is stored by the provider so the URL keeps working.

What's stored where

  • The image file is uploaded directly from your browser to the configured storage provider (for example, Cloudinary). This site does not run its own server that retains a copy.
  • Upload history (filename, URL, upload date, and a thumbnail reference) is saved only in your browser's local storage, on your device. It is never transmitted anywhere else. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Analytics

Analytics are off by default in this deployment (CONFIG.analytics.enabled = false). If the site owner later enables an analytics provider, it will be named here, and it will never involve collecting the contents of uploaded images.

Cookies

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies by default.

Third parties

Uploads are sent to the configured storage provider under that provider's own privacy policy and terms. Review your provider's policy (for example, Cloudinary's) for details on how they handle stored files.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact address on the About page.
